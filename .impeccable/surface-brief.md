# Surface: Home, /menu, /menu/[table]  (mode: persuade for home, operate for table menu; code-led)

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the order slip a café hands you: one cream docket on a dark roast counter. Refuses the generic café hero + card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Roast ground #1c1714; the slip in cream #f1e8d8 with roast ink, deep-matcha stamps, deep-ember only for warnings; Fraunces for shop/slip headers, IBM Plex Sans with tabular numerals for lines and prices; dotted leaders, perforated/zig-zag torn edge, rubber-stamp marks.
STORY: Seated customer scans QR, sees "Table 7" on a slip, finds a drink and price in one thumb-scroll, taps to add to a personal slip, shows staff. Nothing is sent or paid on site.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: slip fills the width with 16px gutters; header (shop name, Jaro, Iloilo City, TABLE N stamp) then the first category lines with leaders to prices; sticky category chips; bottom bar "Your slip" with the primary action. Home: slip with shop name, three-line preview, "View the full menu" stamp button.
FORM: Kitchen docket / order slip, pick 2 of 3 dealt (bag label, docket, flavour wheel). Seed ffe6506e.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
